Reading the analysis / 05

Three ways to explore.

Use these lenses as a guide when examining the charts. The dashboard is descriptive and should not be treated as a clinical diagnostic tool.

LENS / 01

Compare patient groups

Use age-group and BMI-category views to compare how measured indicators differ across segments in the dataset.

LENS / 02

Explore lifestyle factors

Review exercise, alcohol and smoking visualizations to understand how lifestyle categories are represented across patient groups.

LENS / 03

Interpret with context

Check labels, units, filters and tooltips before drawing conclusions. Observed associations do not by themselves establish causation.
